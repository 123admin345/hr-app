# Maternity leave implementation scope

Requested company rule: three calendar months beginning on the actual delivery date, including that day. The calendar return date is the third-month anniversary, clamped to the last day of that month if the original day does not exist. The last leave day is the day before that return date. Saturdays, Fridays and public holidays are included; the app does not convert this to 90 working days or exactly 12 weeks. HR confirms the first scheduled working day if the calendar return date is non-working.

Official HRSD Article 151 currently describes 12 weeks of fully paid maternity leave, six mandatory weeks after delivery, allocation of the remaining weeks including before delivery, a one-month unpaid extension, and provisions for a sick child or child with special needs. Three calendar months is the user's company rule, not a quotation of the statutory minimum. This module does not handle pre-delivery allocation, special statutory extensions, payroll/payment calculation, or establish that the employer can refuse a statutory entitlement. HR should verify policy/legal compliance and handle those cases separately.

Supporting proof must be uploaded in the existing private HR System Slack DM as a PDF or image. The app checks that a file/link was received, not the medical/document contents, legitimacy, or date match. Assigned reviewers must verify the entered delivery date against the document. File access and retention follow Slack/HR Sheet permissions; the module does not create public links or automatically broaden file sharing.

Source read 2026-11-12: https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/knowledge-centre/articles/64410 — Women's Leaves, Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development, Article 151.
